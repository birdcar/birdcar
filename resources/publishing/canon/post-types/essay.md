# Post type: essay or argument

Job: change how an owner sees a problem and leave them with a first move.

Length: 1,500 to 2,500 words.

Structure:

1. The claim, and the answer finished in 40 to 60 words.
2. The scene as evidence.
3. The named mechanism: why the problem keeps happening.
4. The strongest objection, in its own question H2.
5. The first move the reader can make.
6. The invitation paragraph.

Frameworks (pick one per post, or a primary plus one for a single section, and read its "When NOT to use" first):

- `frameworks/the-false-start.md` for a plausible default that fails ("we'll just hire someone").
- `frameworks/the-sparkline.md` for change advocacy. State the "what could be" in plain terms, never as a number.
- `frameworks/the-dialectic.md` for a rebuttal of popular advice.
- `frameworks/the-jeremiad.md` for a practice a trade claims to value and fails to honor. Aim the indictment at the software and consulting trade and at received advice, never at the owner. Needs 1,500 or more words.
- `frameworks/the-montaigne.md` for values pieces (people over metrics, no surveillance).
- Avoid the Sisyphean Arc (it denies resolution) and the Comedian's Set (it does not hold up in prose).
