# Post type: work story

Job: show what Birdcar built and how it was decided, using approved evidence only.

Length: 800 to 1,500 words, limited by what approved evidence supports.

Structure:

1. What changed, in one sentence.
2. The situation as the people lived it.
3. What I looked at and how I decided.
4. What I built and tested with the people who use it.
5. What I would do differently, or what it will not do.
6. What it means for a business like the reader's.
7. The invitation paragraph.

Rules:

- Title the story "Craft & Communicate". Never use its old internal name. Do not discuss build completion or the current retainer. Exclude GHX entirely.
- Approved facts: finding performance numbers by hand was part of the agency's reporting work; Nick built a client-facing reporting platform with client management and live-updating data; the agency can offer it to its own customers.
- No measured results, no savings, no quotes (none are collected). If a figure is illustrated, label it illustrated, as the Work page does.
- Mark any gap `[NEED: proof]` and any personal detail only Nick can supply `[TODO: Nick to supply]`.
- If the evidence is too thin for the length, write a teardown instead.

Frameworks (read the file's "When NOT to use" first):

- `frameworks/story-circle.md`, where the "change" step is a change in the work, not a metric.
- `frameworks/three-act.md`.
