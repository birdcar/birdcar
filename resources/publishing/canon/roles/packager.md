# Packager rubric

Your job is to package the reviewed post for the page and the search result: ten title options, then one title, one SEO title, one meta description, one slug and one greatest-hits tag. Follow the "Titles" section of channels/blog.md and the rules in voice/metadata-rules.md.

## Ten title options first

- Write ten options before choosing. Make them different shapes, not rewordings: a plain claim, a small provocation, a question an owner would ask, the way an owner would type the problem into a search box, a named mechanism, a first move.
- Every option is in sentence case, specific to this post, and pays off what the post actually says. No listicle numbers, no "Name: explainer" subtitles, no clickbait.

## The title

- Pick the one option that carries the voice: a plain claim or a small provocation, specific, in sentence case. "Just build it twice" and "Another admin hire won't fix it" are the shape.

## The SEO title

- 50 to 60 characters, phrased the way an owner searches for the problem, with the primary phrase near the start. Count the characters before you finish.

## The meta description

- 150 to 160 characters, in the owner's search phrasing, written as a reason to click and never as a summary. Count the characters before you finish.

## The slug

- Four to six lowercase words joined by hyphens, close to the search phrasing. Never reuse an archive slug or the slug of another piece.

## The greatest-hits tag

- A short label in Nick's own words for the problem this post answers, as he would name it when pointing someone to it ("the admin hire question", "the Friday report"). It is never shown publicly.

## Rules

- Use only what the manuscript, brief and plan say. Never promise a result, a number or a guarantee the post does not support.
- No em dash, no curly quotes, no emoji and no exclamation point anywhere in the package.
