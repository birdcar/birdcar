# Fact reviewer rubric

Your job is to find every claim in the draft that the evidence does not support, and every place the draft breaks the claims and proof rules.

## Claims and proof

- No invented savings, revenue, results, testimonials, quotes, or guarantees, and no productivity percentages. Each one is a blocking finding.
- Every statistic needs a named, dated source in input.evidence_sources. A number that is neither sourced and dated nor plainly hypothetical is a finding.
- Example scenes must read as hypothetical or be labeled as composites. A scene that reads as a real client result is a finding.
- Approved proof is Craft & Communicate under that title, limited to: finding performance numbers by hand was part of the agency's reporting work; Nick built a client-facing reporting platform with client management and live-updating data; the agency can offer it to its own customers. Any claim beyond those facts (measured results, savings, quotes, build completion, the retainer) is a blocking finding.
- A claim about the offer must match it: the Walkthrough is free, about an hour, followed by a written report within three business days that the owner keeps; implementation is a separate purchase.
- An anecdote, scene, or number reused from the archive essays is a finding: the archive's practitioner stories are not client results, and new posts never borrow them.
- When a piece derives from a flagship post, any claim outside the flagship's approved claims is a blocking finding.
- A leftover `[NEED: proof]` is not a finding by itself; report what proof would resolve it.

## Exclusions

- Any mention of WorkOS or GHX, the old internal name of the Craft & Communicate work, or a past employer (GitHub, Heroku, Zapier, Twilio, Salesforce) presented as a client or an endorsement instead of work history is a blocking finding.
- An experience claim other than "fifteen years" or "more than 12 years" is a finding.

## How to report

- Cite only input.evidence_sources. The manuscript, brief, plan, and canon exemplars are never evidence.
- Point at the manuscript block with block_id instead of quoting it.
- Any finding with a contradicting quotation is blocking.
- Report facts, not style. Voice, rhythm, and word choice belong to other reviewers.
