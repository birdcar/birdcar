Owners of established service businesses (referrals, outreach, 1 Million Cups) recognize their own overloaded desk, understand what I build and that the free Walkthrough starts it, and book. ## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Show owners their own business in miniature, every task landing on their desk, then show it fixed. Refuses the consultancy hero-plus-services-grid and the SaaS gradient-and-bento default.

**OWN-WORLD:** Pale daylight #eef3f1, ink #122127, canary yellow #f5c542 for actions and packets, teal-cyan floors. Premium matte isometric studio renders, glowing packets on dotted paths, white pill tags, heavy tight grotesk, one Alkaline wordmark, a canary.

**STORY:** The owner sees the pile on their desk, watches work reroute to the people doing it, learns what I build and that a free Walkthrough starts it, and books.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Approved comp 1. “Better work, faster. Without another hire.” ~72px left; subline, yellow button, terms. Cutaway business right, packets converging on the owner's desk. Past-employer row below the fold line.

**FORM:** Your business, in miniature; my #1 grounded candidate; seed 8448c8f7.

## Content and behavior

Signature interaction: packets travel from desks and pile on the owner's desk; the diorama pins on scroll through three states. Walkthrough crossfades the owner's office to the cage open and the canary on the desk facing the owner, laptop closed, the two talking it through; desk tags become the shared pattern names. After the fix, work reroutes into lanes between the people doing it, the stack clears, the warning light goes out, and the canary sits on top of the open cage. No flying sprite and no narrating caption (owner direction, 2026-09-25). Desks are clickable toys that name what is stuck there, using the shared pattern vocabulary. Every relationship stays readable with no JavaScript, reduced motion, and print. The canary, dry wit, and small toys carry personality; no generic AI imagery. Keep `hero`, `homepage-strip`, and `closing-invitation` booking placements, the `#how-i-work` anchor, native mobile menu, and real booking fallbacks. No portrait yet; leave room for one in the person section.
