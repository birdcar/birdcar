## Visitor and first action

An owner or operations lead of an established service business should recognize
their coordination burden, meet the person who can help, understand that person's judgment
and approach, and book a free discovery assessment. A practical written report
they can keep makes the first step concrete. Follow `PRODUCT.md` for the offer
and its limits.

## Personal presence and copy

The owner's voice, vision, expertise, and direct involvement are the central
offering. Introduce the person in the first viewport and sustain that relationship
through explanations, work stories, and the booking invitation. Use I, me, my.
No corporate we, us, or our. Explain collaboration as working with the client
and their team. Apply the essay-derived voice guidance in `PRODUCT.md`.

The selected preview predates this explicit voice correction. Its headline
“Make room for better work.” remains a directional expression, not an approved
copy deck. Replace its impersonal supporting copy during copy development.
An initial draft to establish the register:

> I like figuring out why work is harder than it needs to be. Usually, that starts
> with the people doing it. Show me what's painful, and I'll help you work out
> what would make it better.

The draft above is new proposed copy, not a quotation or approved final wording.
Keep the free assessment and take-away report explicit near the main action.
The illustration must depict collaboration with a client's team; do not imply
that Birdcar employs the pictured people. Determine personal imagery from actual
available assets, without fabricating a portrait or customer scene.

## Evidence and continuation

Writing is a first-class destination in the main marketing navigation. Include
a blog index and individual article pages in the marketing scope, with room for
future video content. A considered selection of essays can show the owner's
thinking from the homepage. Use actual authored content; the selected comp's
navigation predates this requirement and must gain a Writing link in the next
composition round. Give the index and reading pages their own briefs when shaped.

Continue into quieter, readable work stories: the completed DataDash build for
Craft & Communicate and the independently delivered GHX GTM workshops. Explain
what I understood, what I did, and what the client received. Distinguish the earlier
joint Mind on Fire engagement. Do not invent quotes or measured results.
Any conceptual illustration is explanatory, not a screenshot or evidence of a client.
