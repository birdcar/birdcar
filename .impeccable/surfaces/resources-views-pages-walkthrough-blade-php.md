# The Walkthrough

## Scope

Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Explain exactly what the free conversation produces and help a referral prospect choose a time, without a sales call disguised as discovery.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** An hour on a real process produces a useful written recommendation, not free implementation.

**OWN-WORLD:** The approved cyan/ink/white Barlow world, a square folded report and yellow first recommendation; single Alkaline header wordmark.

**STORY:** Understand the deliverable → bring a real example → follow the sequence → resolve fair questions → book.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Cyan opening; large concrete offer left with yellow booking action; report contents right, with the first recommendation emphasized and timing explicit.

**FORM:** The clear argument, approved grounded pick, seed `d5125406`; page-specific extension, not a new visual world.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Content and behavior

Roughly one hour, free and pitch-free, written report within three business days. Observed problems, recommendations, and Where I’d start. Implementation is an optional separate purchase, visible outside the FAQs. Keep one unpriced paid-discovery-week sentence. Fit requires access to the people doing the work. No invented outcomes, scarcity, VSL, or promise of free implementation.

All booking CTAs on this page lead to `#choose-a-time`; inline Cal uses namespace `walkthrough`, with the plain Cal.com event fallback. Native FAQ disclosures work without JavaScript; existing menu/focus/reduced-motion behavior remains. On narrow screens, offer precedes report, then chronological meeting steps, questions, and calendar. No custom second animation.
