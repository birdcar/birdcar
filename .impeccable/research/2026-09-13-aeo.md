# AEO foundations for the Birdcar redesign

Research and source-code audit: 2026-09-13. This note recommends implementation; it does not claim the deployed domain has been audited or changed.

## Recommendation

Build a site that a business owner can understand, a search crawler can retrieve, and a browser agent can use. Preserve the approved offer, original essays, and direct first-person voice. Make animation an enhancement to complete server-rendered content. A type-led design is compatible with this; the typography must remain actual text and the copy must not wait for a scroll trigger or JavaScript to become available.

This is an implementation inference from the official guidance below, not a promise of ranking, recommendation, or citation in ChatGPT or Claude.

## What the primary sources establish

| System | Search discovery | Training | User-directed access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAI | `OAI-SearchBot` surfaces sites in ChatGPT search. OpenAI recommends allowing it and its published IP ranges. | `GPTBot` concerns potential foundation-model training. Its robots setting is independent of search. | `ChatGPT-User` handles some user-initiated visits. It is not the search crawler, and robots.txt may not apply to these requests. |
| Anthropic | `Claude-SearchBot` improves search relevance and indexing. | `ClaudeBot` collects material that could contribute to training. | `Claude-User` retrieves pages in response to user requests. Anthropic states its bots honor robots.txt. |

Sources: [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Anthropic crawler documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler).

Google's current guide centers useful original material and a clear technical foundation. It expressly says that Google Search ignores `llms.txt`; special AI files, artificial content chunking, and rewriting solely for AI are unnecessary. Structured data is not a special generative-search requirement. Treat crawlability and good editorial work as the foundation, and measure actual results rather than expecting placement. The current guide links a Generative AI performance report in Search Console. [Google's AI optimization guide, updated July 10, 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

Structured data must accurately represent visible content. Use JSON-LD for maintainable machine-readable facts, without invented identity details, proof, ratings, or claims. [Google structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies).

FAQ rich results stopped appearing in Google Search on May 7, 2026; the feature documentation was removed in June. Keep the assessment questions because they answer buyer objections. FAQ markup is optional semantic description, not a Google rich-result goal. [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

Robots.txt is a crawler directive, not access control or a dependable way to keep a URL out of search. Protect private content with authentication and authorization. Use `noindex` on accessible pages that should not be indexed; blocking the crawler can prevent it from seeing that directive. [Google's developer guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/get-started-developers).

## Existing strengths and concrete gaps

| Area | Evidence in this checkout | Narrow implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content delivery | `resources/views/pages/*.blade.php` and `app/Actions/ReadWriting.php` render marketing and articles on the server. Navigation and FAQ use native links/details. | Keep all headings, answers, article prose, and booking links usable without JS. Add motion only after initial usable HTML; respect reduced motion. |
| Metadata | `resources/views/components/marketing/layout.blade.php` already has title, description, canonical, and Open Graph metadata. `resources/views/pages/writing/[slug].blade.php` preserves original dates, byline, and trailing-slash canonical. | Retain and test this. Canonicals currently derive from the request host or host-dependent named routes: establish one configured production public origin while allowing local previews. Keep archived URL paths. |
| Entity and offer | No JSON-LD found. Public identity is Birdcar, a person, and the assessment plainly states free conversation/report without a required implementation purchase. | Add a small linked `Person`/`WebSite` graph, an assessment `Service` referencing the person, and article `BlogPosting` metadata with original publication dates. Use only already-visible facts; do not invent legal name, address, regions served, pricing for paid work, or customer outcomes. |
| Sitemap | `routes/web.php` has RSS and old-path redirects; no sitemap route or file found. `ReadWriting::all()` already excludes drafts and future posts. | Add an XML sitemap for the public marketing pages and published essays using that same published collection. Exclude admin/customer/auth/tool routes, redirects, unknown essays, and drafts. Include only truthful modification dates, or omit them. |
| Crawl policy | `public/robots.txt` currently allows every agent and contains no sitemap declaration. | Publish the sitemap location and keep public pages available to search agents. Do not silently turn a search-discovery task into a training opt-in/out change; current wildcard permission can be preserved unless the owner chooses otherwise. |
| Surface separation | `app/Providers/FolioServiceProvider.php` registers marketing without a domain constraint. `routes/web.php` defines an authenticated, permission-gated admin root and empty customer route groups. No `noindex`/`X-Robots-Tag` policy found. | Verify host behavior and avoid marketing copies with tenant-host canonicals. Keep actual private data behind auth/policies. Add noindex response policy for nonpublic surfaces and preview environments; ensure robots responses are correct per host. Empty customer scaffolding is not proof of exposed customer data. |
| Original expertise | Homepage recognizes manual reporting, memory-dependent follow-up, and work repeatedly returning to the owner. Writing keeps ten original essays; assessment contains six concrete buyer questions. | Keep the existing copy. Future business-focused essays can answer those operational problems with the owner's firsthand reasoning, examples, tradeoffs, and approved evidence. Do not mass-generate keyword pages or repurpose archive essays to sell the offer. |

Schema vocabulary references: [Person](https://schema.org/Person), [Service](https://schema.org/Service), [BlogPosting](https://schema.org/BlogPosting). These types describe facts; they do not establish how any answer engine ranks the site.

## Focused verification when implemented

Extend `tests/Feature/MarketingSiteTest.php` or add a focused metadata test alongside it:

- Parse the rendered JSON-LD and assert the real public entity, offer, canonical URL, article title, and original date; ensure forbidden proof such as GHX remains absent.
- Parse sitemap XML and assert only canonical public URLs and published articles appear. Exercise draft/future exclusions and the nonpublic-host boundary.
- Assert canonical/OG agreement under ordinary requests and query parameters, with explicit production-origin behavior.
- Verify nonpublic responses remain protected and receive noindex where intended; do not weaken existing authorization tests.
- Inspect desktop/mobile once with JS disabled and reduced motion: actual content, navigation, FAQ answers, and booking remain usable. Keep animation from trapping content at opacity zero after failure.

Existing tests already cover approved offer/proof, ten original essay URLs/dates, prose and link preservation, accessible chart data, unknown-post 404s, unsafe Markdown, RSS, and redirects. Preserve this coverage.

Production follow-through remains a deployment check: public 200 responses, real redirects, robots/sitemap delivery, CDN/WAF crawler access, and Search Console inspection. Local code alone cannot establish those conditions or actual AI visibility.
